# Proposed System

## Introduction

The Product Warranty Claim Processing Platform is a web-based application that automates the entire warranty claim process. It provides a centralized system for customers, administrators, and service centers to manage warranty claims efficiently.

## Proposed Process

1. Customer registers an account.
2. Customer registers purchased products.
3. Invoice and warranty details are uploaded.
4. Customer submits a warranty claim.
5. Administrator verifies claim details.
6. Claim is assigned to the appropriate service center.
7. Service center updates repair or replacement status.
8. Customer tracks claim status in real time.
9. Administrator generates reports and monitors system performance.

## Advantages

- Fully digital warranty management.
- Faster claim verification.
- Secure storage of invoices and warranty records.
- Real-time claim tracking.
- Automated notifications.
- Better communication between all users.
- Centralized database management.
- Reduced paperwork.
- Improved transparency.
- Better reporting and analytics.

## Modules

- User Management
- Product Registration
- Warranty Verification
- Claim Management
- Service Center Management
- Notification System
- Reports & Analytics
- Admin Dashboard

## Conclusion

The proposed system improves efficiency, reduces manual work, increases transparency, and provides a better customer experience through an automated warranty claim management process.

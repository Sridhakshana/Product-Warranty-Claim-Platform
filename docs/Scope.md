# Scope

## Project Scope

The Product Warranty Claim Processing Platform is designed to automate the warranty claim process for electronic products and appliances. The system provides a centralized platform where customers, administrators, and service centers can manage warranty-related activities efficiently.

## In Scope

- User Registration and Login
- Role-Based Access Control
- Product Registration
- Warranty Information Management
- Invoice Upload
- Warranty Claim Submission
- Claim Verification
- Claim Approval and Rejection
- Service Center Assignment
- Claim Status Tracking
- Notifications
- Dashboard and Reports
- Secure Database Management

## Out of Scope

- Online Product Purchase
- Payment Gateway Integration
- Product Delivery Tracking
- Inventory Management
- Manufacturing Process Management

## Future Scope

- AI-based Invoice Verification
- OCR for Automatic Invoice Data Extraction
- QR Code Warranty Verification
- Mobile Application
- Predictive Analytics
- Cloud Deployment
- Chatbot Support

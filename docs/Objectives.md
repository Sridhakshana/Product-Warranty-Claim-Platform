# Objectives

## Primary Objective

To develop a web-based Product Warranty Claim Processing Platform that automates the warranty claim process and provides a secure, transparent, and efficient system for customers, administrators, and service centers.

## Specific Objectives

- Develop a secure user authentication system.
- Implement role-based access control (Customer, Admin, Service Center).
- Enable customers to register products and upload invoices.
- Allow customers to submit warranty claims online.
- Provide real-time claim status tracking.
- Allow administrators to verify, approve, or reject claims.
- Assign claims to appropriate service centers.
- Maintain complete warranty and claim history.
- Generate reports and analytics.
- Reduce paperwork and manual processing.
- Improve customer satisfaction.

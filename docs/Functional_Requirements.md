# Functional Requirements

## User Management

- Users can register and log in securely.
- Users can update their profile information.
- Role-based access control for Customer, Admin, and Service Center.

## Product Management

- Customers can register purchased products.
- Customers can upload invoices.
- Customers can view registered products.

## Warranty Management

- Store warranty details for each product.
- Verify warranty validity automatically.
- Display warranty expiry date.

## Claim Management

- Submit warranty claims.
- Upload supporting documents.
- Track claim status.
- View claim history.

## Admin Management

- View all claims.
- Approve or reject claims.
- Assign claims to service centers.
- Manage users and products.

## Service Center Management

- View assigned claims.
- Update repair status.
- Mark claims as completed.

## Notification System

- Notify customers when claim status changes.
- Notify service centers when a claim is assigned.

## Reports

- Generate warranty reports.
- Generate claim reports.
- View dashboard statistics.

# Problem Statement

## Title

Product Warranty Claim Processing Platform

## Background

Many organizations still process product warranty claims manually through paper forms, emails, or phone calls. This approach often causes delays, data inconsistency, poor communication, and difficulty in tracking warranty claims. Customers also face challenges in checking the status of their claims and maintaining warranty documents.

## Problem

The existing warranty claim process has several limitations:

- Manual verification of warranty details.
- Difficulty in tracking claim status.
- Loss of invoices or warranty documents.
- Delayed approval process.
- Lack of transparency between customers and service centers.
- Poor record management.
- Increased workload for administrators.

## Proposed Solution

The Product Warranty Claim Processing Platform provides a centralized web application where customers can register products, upload invoices, submit warranty claims, and track claim status in real time. Administrators can verify claims, assign service centers, approve or reject requests, and generate reports. The system improves efficiency, transparency, and customer satisfaction.

## Expected Outcome

- Faster warranty claim processing.
- Better customer experience.
- Reduced manual work.
- Secure digital record management.
- Real-time claim tracking.
- Improved communication among customers, administrators, and service centers.

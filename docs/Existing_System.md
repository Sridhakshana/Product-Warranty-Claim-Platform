# Existing System

## Introduction

Most organizations use traditional methods for handling product warranty claims. Customers usually submit warranty requests through paper forms, emails, or by visiting service centers directly. The verification process is mostly manual, making the overall system slow and inefficient.

## Existing Process

1. Customer purchases a product.
2. Customer keeps the physical invoice and warranty card.
3. Warranty claim is submitted manually.
4. Administrator verifies product details.
5. Service center checks warranty eligibility.
6. Claim is approved or rejected.
7. Customer receives the final response.

## Limitations

- Manual data entry.
- Time-consuming verification process.
- Poor communication between customers and service centers.
- Difficult to track claim status.
- Risk of losing warranty documents.
- Duplicate claim possibilities.
- No centralized database.
- Limited reporting and analytics.
- High administrative workload.
- Less transparency in the process.

## Conclusion

The existing system lacks automation, security, and real-time tracking. These limitations reduce customer satisfaction and increase operational costs. Therefore, a digital warranty claim processing platform is required.
